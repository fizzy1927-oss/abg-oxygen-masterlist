    Mock Exam

    A timed, board-style test that pulls from every quiz on the site. No answers are shown until you submit, just like the real exam. Then you get a score by area and a full review.

    The NBRC TMC has 160 questions in 3 hours (about 67 seconds each); its two cut scores have been about 86 and 92 of 140 scored questions (≈ 61% and 66%). The NBRC’s new RT exam, starting January 2027, has 185 questions in 4 hours. Check nbrc.org for the current details. This mock exam is practice only and isn’t affiliated with the NBRC.
