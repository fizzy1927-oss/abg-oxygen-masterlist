    How to read an ABG
    1. pH: below 7.35 is acidemia, above 7.45 is alkalemia.
    2. PaCO₂ (the lungs): high is acid, low is base. If it matches the pH, the problem is respiratory.
    3. HCO₃⁻ (the kidneys): low is acid, high is base. If it matches the pH, the problem is metabolic.
    4. Compensation: the other value is normal → uncompensated. It moved the opposite way and the pH is still abnormal → partially compensated. The pH is back to normal → fully compensated (the side of 7.40 the pH sits on tells you which problem came first).
    5. Oxygenation: PaO₂ 80–100 is normal, 60–79 mild, 40–59 moderate, below 40 severe hypoxemia.

    ROME: Respiratory Opposite (pH and PaCO₂ move opposite ways), Metabolic Equal (pH and HCO₃⁻ move the same way).
